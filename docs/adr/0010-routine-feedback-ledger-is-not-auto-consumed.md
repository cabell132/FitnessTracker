# Routine feedback ledger is not auto-consumed

Routine feedback reviews are artifact-first, and accepted classifications are persisted to a Routine feedback ledger so Athlete edits are not lost after review. Routine creation does not automatically consume ledger entries in the first version: promotion to prescription-shaping behavior requires explicit review because a Routine edit may represent a generation defect, durable preference, session-only substitution, or performed-result noise. This trades autonomy for auditability while preserving evidence for future defaults and repair work.
